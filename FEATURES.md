# Nishaani capability inventory

These are implemented browser-demo capabilities, not claims of a hosted archival service. The starter collection is fictional. Some items below are separate user actions within a workflow so the inventory makes the available surface clear.

## Reading room and navigation

1. Seven hash-routed pages: Reading room, Story catalogue, New testimony, Transcript desk, Rights & consent, Exhibitions, and Archive care.
2. Responsive desktop sidebar and mobile slide-in navigation.
3. Persistent masthead with archive identity and local-workspace status.
4. Reading-room collection count.
5. Featured sample story with a direct story-detail action.
6. Recent stewardship activity list.
7. Quick navigation from page headings to capture.
8. Search dialog with keyboard-accessible input.
9. Search across public-excerpt titles, places, phrases, languages, and themes.
10. Restricted records excluded from global search.

## Story catalogue and records

11. Eight fictional Thane-area sample records.
12. Theme filters for all stories and eight sample themes.
13. Live text search over visible catalogue metadata.
14. Empty-results state with guidance.
15. Record cards show place and period.
16. Record cards show the current access label.
17. Record cards show pseudonym, language, and duration metadata.
18. Story detail view with excerpt and transcript.
19. Provenance fields for source context and record date.
20. Transcript revision count displayed on a story.
21. Private story access guard in catalogue and public-facing views.
22. One-click jump from a story detail into its transcript.
23. One-click jump from a story detail into rights review.
24. Single-record JSON export.

57. Add a draft title and description.

59. Filter restricted stories out of exhibition preview output.
60. View archive counts for records, transcript revisions, exhibition drafts, and local storage.
61. Export a JSON archive backup with timestamp and schema label.
62. Restore from a JSON archive backup after confirmation.
63. Restore the fictional starter collection after confirmation.
64. Maintain a capped local activity history for archive actions.
65. Show clear local-only and no-publication status messaging.

## Demo boundaries

The local rights label does not create, verify, or replace consent. No action contacts a speaker or publishes content. There is no account system, remote database, encryption layer, transcription model, AI-generated interpretation, or multi-user workflow. Backups omit audio. Do not put real sensitive testimony into this prototype until a secure governance and storage setup exists.
